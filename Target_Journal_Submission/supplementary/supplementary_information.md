The continuous linear spline was locked as primary before any panel run. Full record and development-set numbers: `supplementary/adcc_preregistration.md` and `code/results/dev_adcc_rows.jsonl`.

## S-adcc-ablation. Function-class ablation

The function class is the only design choice. On the held-out panel, the indicator class reproduces Mondrian; the spline (primary) and piecewise-linear classes give the best novel-compound coverage and calibration; the normalized-score spline trades width/saturation for marginal gains (table S-adcc-panel). This confirms the class, not incidental tuning, drives the result.

## S-adcc-leak. Data-leakage audit

For every target the audit asserts chronological monotonicity (max train/calib year ≤ min test year up to boundary ties), InChIKey de-duplication, applicability-domain scores computed to the training set only, and that no labels enter featurization, AD scores or the (baseline) weight estimators. See `code/experiments/leakage_audit.py` and `code/results/leakage_audit_panel.json`.

## S-prior. Prior estimated-weight benchmark

The estimated-weight methods ADR-CP and MW-CP, and the earlier multi-target benchmark that motivated this work (including the CoDrug-/KMM-style weightings and the cross-target diagnostic analysis), are retained in the released code and summarized in the main text. ADCC-CP matches or exceeds their decision-relevant coverage while removing the density-ratio estimate and adding a finite-sample guarantee.
