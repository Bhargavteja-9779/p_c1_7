## Synthesis
- **What exists:** (a) distribution-free CP is standard for QSAR UQ/AD (Norinder, Svensson, Cortés-Ciriano, Alvarsson, Xu, Li 2024); (b) its coverage **breaks under scaffold/temporal shift** (Morger 2022, Hosni 2026, Guo 2027); (c) **marginal** coverage can be **restored** by covariate-shift weighting — but only demonstrated for **classification** with a GNN+KDE pipeline (CoDrug 2023); (d) recent regression benchmarks (Guo 2027) show marginal repair does **not** imply **conditional** (AD-stratified) repair, and the practical similarity-adaptive CP variants they tried do not fix it; (e) domain classifiers are known OOD signals (Harris 2026) but used as *filters*, not coverage-correcting weights.
- **What is missing (the gap):** a **regression**, **model-agnostic**, **deployment-ready** conformal method that uses the **unlabeled deployment pool** to correct covariate shift and that is evaluated on, and designed for, **conditional (applicability-domain-stratified) coverage** across **scaffold and temporal** shift on **multiple datasets** with repeated-split statistics — together with the **scientific characterization** of *when marginal correction suffices and when conditional correction is required*.
